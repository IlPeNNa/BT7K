There *is* no game more beautiful than an isometric game. Thank you for buying "Ancient Isometic Tileset Volume I". With this set you can get started building scenes with castles, dungeons, towns with a classic fantasy medieval setting.

ABOUT and a general opinion on isometric tiles

Isometric is just complicated. There is no perfect solution and some compromises must be made.

The tiles are designed so that most tiles will work in a simple one level isometric engine. Extra detail comes in the form of decals or "splat" images that are used to make the word look more detailed.

The tiles work within the Tiled editor and the sample is given as a tiled tileset and example levels. So, if your engine works in the same way as Tiled, these tiles will work for your engine.

Included is an isometric projected grid. The projection used is 2:1, available template on https://opengameart.org/content/isometric-grid-pack.

Tile images are either 512x256 (e.g. walls, ground, etc) or free form sized (e.g. roofs, trees, etc).

Heights for tiles inside the images such as walls, are 160x128 in order to present a clear appearance.

Some tiles, like building cubes are 128x128 which are intended for consistent stacking with smooth texture repeating without overlap.

So, an option if you want to build a tall structure is to use the 128x128 tiles and on the top level use the 160x128 tiles.

With some extra effort for arranging tiles into layers, interiors are an option, as well as interior multi-story structures.

For some isometric solutions, the simplest option may be to create your own sliced up tile. There is a sample tile corner_w to demonstrate this in Interior_2 directory.

Likewise, for roofs you may wish to modify the tiles and reposition the images.
